DESCRICAO (YouTube, em ingles)
Eleven meters below the Egyptian desert, archaeologists found a narrow burial shaft at Saqqara filled with sealed coffins from the Late Period, about 2,500 years old. In this video we walk through what was reported, what the official images actually show, and what still has not been proven.

This video does NOT claim to know who was buried there, and it treats every date and count as reported, not as a final scientific result.
